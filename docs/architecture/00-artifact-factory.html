The mechanism: what causes each stage change

Figure 1. The nine-stage lane machine and the private-test boundary. Each arrow is labelled with the artifact or verdict that causes it, taken from the transition table in §3 of the factory contract. The dashed zone is the private half: the tester and tester-reviewer work in trees outside the product repository, and sealed bytes go to the vault. Exactly one edge leaves that zone into BUILDING, and the gate on it names everything the builder receives and everything it does not — a builder that could read the suite would be writing to the test, so the boundary is the reason a PASS means anything. Only two things ever come back across it: TEST_INVALIDATION carrying a redacted reason for a private-path collision, and the redacted findings inside a CODE_REVIEW. The code reviewer, not the builder, runs the sealed suite in scratch, and that broker result is authoritative for the candidate.

Where the durable state lives

Persistent role topology. Lanes are linked children of the Space Herdr names as the repository's source checkout (worktree list --cwd <repo> → source_workspace_id), normally the operator's own; Maestro creates its own <repo>-<4hash> Space only when none is open there. Linked children are labelled the exact authored lane_id and created only when that lane first dispatches. Role panes are lazy: tester, tester-reviewer, builder, code-reviewer, and last-topo integration-reviewer. Stable identity is (project, run, lane, role). Every started role owns a stable role-scoped cwd and retains its pane/session context through its review or revision loop and scheduler restart. The tester and tester-reviewer use distinct role-private trees; the builder's and code-reviewer's respective cwds are reused through BUILDING ↔ REVIEWING_CODE. Run-level final review and publication use the last-topo integration-reviewer—there is no integration tab. Rediscovery accepts only exact Herdr report-metadata identity (full run_id, repository fingerprint, lane_id, role, parent Space ID) plus live IDs, parent relationship, pane cwd, agent ownership, stable agent name, and role label. Only confirmed dead or agent_not_found roles may be recreated; live, unknown, malformed, mismatched, dirty, or unapproved observations are refused. Legacy stage- or attempt-named panes are never adopted or renamed. After MAIN_PUBLICATION and derived COMPLETE: idle proof, /rename to <repo>-<4hash>-<lane_id>-<role>, exact confirmation, then close the lane children. Pane state is transport, never workflow authority. Persistent role topology diagram.
